Preclinical testing of anti-inflammatory compounds in models of RGC degeneration

Anti-inflammatory compounds tested in vivo in models of retinal ganglion cell degeneration. Pick any condition on the left as the wheel's entry point, narrow it with filters, and open the studies behind each mark.

Conditions

Each radial slice is one experiment and keeps its angle in every ring. Use “Wheel” on any condition, or pick an option in the sorting wedge at the top, to sort the wheel by it; the other rings line up as a colour bar code. Hover a segment or mark to light up those experiments everywhere; click it to list the studies.

Data: the 76 in vivo experiments listed in Tables 1–3 of Malahov et al., “Pharmacological targeting of TNF-α, IL-1β and IL-6 signaling in retinal ganglion cell degeneration” (manuscript under review). One experiment = one study × compound × model/species × route. Treatment duration counts days from first to last dose, inclusive (continuous dosing until sacrifice; several arms = longest). Reference numbers follow the manuscript. “No RGC readout” marks studies that reported inflammatory or structural endpoints without RGC counts.
